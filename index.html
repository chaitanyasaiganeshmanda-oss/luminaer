Customer help center

Use your essentials thoughtfully

Hand hygiene

Soap and water are preferred when available. Use sanitizer as labelled and keep it away from flames.

Toothbrush storage

Rinse after use, allow the brush to dry and avoid sealing it while wet.

Surface cleaning

Use disinfectant only on compatible surfaces and follow label directions.

UV safety

Never expose skin, eyes, people or pets to UV-C. Use only an enclosed, safety-interlocked device.
