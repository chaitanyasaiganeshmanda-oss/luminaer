01 / THE IDEA

Small essentials.
One smarter place.

LUMINAER is a proposed portable personal-hygiene kit that brings frequently used care items together. Instead of carrying several loose products, students can keep everyday essentials organised and easier to reach.

It is designed with hostel residents, shared-accommodation students, commuters and travellers in mind.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
Answered.

What is LUMINAER?

A proposed portable personal-hygiene kit designed around the everyday needs of students, especially hostel residents.

Is it available to purchase?

This website presents a student innovation concept. Availability, final specifications and pricing have not been established.

Who is it designed for?

College students, hostel residents, commuters and travellers.

Can I store wet items inside?

Dry wet items before storage whenever possible. Separate damp and dry items to prevent trapped moisture.

Does it use UV-C sanitization?

An optional UV-C module is a proposed concept only. No sanitization performance or safety certification is claimed. UV-C requires full enclosure, effective safety interlocks and independent testing.
